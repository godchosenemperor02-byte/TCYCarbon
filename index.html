FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know about placing a custom build order and getting it delivered.

Do you do custom makes for any car model? +

Yes, absolutely! We build handcrafted wheels for virtually any vehicle make and model. Whether you have a BMW, Audi, Porsche, Mustang, Golf R, or a classic chassis, send us your car details, colors, or sketch idea, and we will custom-wrap and tailor a wheel engineered specifically for your car.

Do you offer worldwide shipping? +

Yes! We ship all of our custom wheels worldwide. Every wheel is packed in custom protective casing to ensure it arrives in pristine condition regardless of your location.

How can I get mine and start a build? +

Getting your custom wheel is simple:

  1. Submit Your Specs: Fill out our configurator above or select a preset build.
  2. Review & Quote: We review your car specs, confirm plug-and-play fitment, and send an exact price quote within 24 hours.
  3. Handcrafted Production: Once confirmed, we build your wheel from raw carbon to final stitching.
  4. Worldwide Delivery: We inspect, package, and ship your finished wheel straight to your door with tracking.
